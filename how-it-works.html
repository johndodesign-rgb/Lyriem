How Do I Get Started?

Four steps to better work.

1

Create Your Profile

Sign up as a Maker (freelancer) or Initiator (business). Your profile becomes your digital identity — verified on-chain and owned by you. Every project you complete or approve builds your permanent, verifiable reputation.

2

Connect & Collaborate

Makers find opportunities and Initiators hire freelancers through open projects. Everything happens directly — no middlemen, no platform interference, no locked messaging systems.

3

Secure Payment in Escrow

Funds move into escrow before work begins. Once milestones are approved, payment is released automatically. Both sides are protected, start to finish.

4

Build Your Reputation

Every completed project strengthens your verified on-chain record. Makers earn proof of skill and reliability. Initiators earn proof of trust and follow-through. Your reputation stays with you — forever.
